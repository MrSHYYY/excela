# Excela

Excela turns class announcements into a Google Sheets planner. Paste a message, choose Gemini or Ollama, and extract the course, event, and date.

Sign in with Google, connect your planner or generate one from the template, then click **Sync with DOC** to add events to the matching dates. New entries appear in dark red with white text, and duplicate entries are skipped.

AI handles extraction only—syncing to Google Sheets uses no AI tokens.
